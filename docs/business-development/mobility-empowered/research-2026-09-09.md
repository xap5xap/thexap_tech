# A product and execution partnership with Alan

The strongest commercial position for Xavier is Alan’s trusted product and execution partner: a technically capable right hand who takes responsibility for an agreed initiative from opportunity assessment through delivery, launch, and learning. The intended purchase is additional ownership capacity. Research, product strategy, design, engineering, AI orchestration, and marketing are the means of delivering that capacity.

The recommended path is to establish the long-term relationship through one paid, bounded first initiative. Alan should be able to see useful progress, understand the evidence behind the decisions, and experience a reduction in the coordination he personally carries. A larger ongoing engagement becomes easier to assess after that demonstration.

This is a strategy proposal for Xavier’s private use. The opportunities, engagement design, schedules, and commercial terms below are recommendations. No particular application, fee, client commitment, or outreach has been selected.

**1. Relationship context and the real buying decision**

Xavier previously helped Alan build CargoEmpowered.com, a consumer moving application using Next.js. Xavier reports that it was released and later retired, after he had left. He suspects insufficient research affected the audience and feature choices; the actual retirement reason is unknown. That distinction matters. The next conversation should invite Alan’s account of what happened rather than build a pitch on an unconfirmed diagnosis.

Xavier describes Alan as the CEO and CTO of a startup, technically strong and knowledgeable about AI. Xavier wants an ongoing position as a reliable right hand for the ideas Alan wants to accomplish. These are the controlling relationship inputs. Whether Alan currently has the budget, organizational need, and desire to delegate such ownership remains to be established.

The buying decision is likely to involve four questions: Is there an important initiative that lacks sufficient ownership? Can Xavier carry it with manageable supervision? Will his work fit Alan’s existing team and technical direction? Can the relationship remain dependable over time? This is an analytical hypothesis about the decision, not a report of Alan’s private concerns.

The proposed position is:

> I can be your product and execution partner: taking an agreed opportunity through research, product decisions, implementation, launch, and learning, while keeping you involved in the decisions that need your judgment.

“Product & Execution Partner” is a useful working label. “Fractional Product Lead” could describe the arrangement if Alan has a role for it. The title should follow the responsibilities and delegated authority they agree. Xavier’s engineering ability remains an advantage: he can connect a recommendation to its implementation and stay responsible when reality changes.

**2. What Mobility Empowered’s public evidence suggests**

Mobility Empowered presents a business serving employers, relocating employees, and suppliers. Its public offering includes housing, moving services, onboarding, reporting, and a supplier network. The site links separate user portals and directs prospective buyers toward a demo. This establishes a multi-party business context; it does not reveal its internal workflow quality or the performance of any funnel.[1]

The company’s LinkedIn positioning combines relocation services with an intelligence platform. A company post about Alan’s EuRA2025 remarks describes AI supporting supply-chain work while people retain judgment and customer relationships. This provides a relevant connection to Xavier’s proposed working method. It also shows that AI is already part of the company’s thinking.[2][3]

The publisher’s RelocateME listing describes an employee-facing mobile experience for relocation information, services, dates, and contacts. Consequently, any proposed onboarding or relocation assistant needs an existing-product audit before a build recommendation. The listing is evidence of a publicly offered application; it does not establish usage, satisfaction, or what happened to CargoEmpowered.[4]

Three implications follow from these observations:

- Alan may value someone who can translate a business priority into a coherent initiative across his product, service, and commercial teams. This is a hypothesis to test with him.
- A first project can build on current customer relationships, staff knowledge, and product surfaces, if access is available. Those may make learning less expensive than recruiting a new consumer audience.
- Reuse, integration, configuration, a process improvement, and a new product should all remain viable solutions until the problem is understood.

The public homepage emphasizes the marketplace, while LinkedIn gives greater emphasis to service and intelligence. That difference suggests a possible messaging question: do these surfaces describe the same current offer clearly for the intended buyer? It is not evidence of a conversion problem. Traffic, prospect questions, sales conversations, and campaign results are needed before recommending a redesign.[1][2]

A small competitor check strengthens the case for specificity. Benivo publicly combines services, software, integrations, and AI-oriented mobility tooling; Topia promotes a platform spanning mobility workflows and agentic AI. These are vendor claims, not independently verified performance findings. They show that a broad AI-mobility promise already appears in the category. An opportunity should be grounded in Mobility Empowered’s particular customers, workflows, and advantages.[5][6]

The public research does not establish company revenue, margins, current staffing gaps, Alan’s backlog, procurement authority beyond Xavier’s description, or a validated need for a new application. The direct CargoEmpowered page and search did not supply usable evidence explaining its retirement. Public pages and cached social profiles vary in freshness; follower counts, headcount, and relative post dates are not used as decision evidence here.

**4. The capability story to tell**

The most persuasive narrative is a change in responsibility:

> Since CargoEmpowered, I’ve developed a much broader way of working. With Armonía I carried the work from understanding the operating problem through product decisions, design, implementation, and launch material. With Vela I’ve built a structured way to coordinate research and delivery with AI. I’d like to bring that ownership to one of your priorities.

This is proposed conversation language, not a message ready for automatic sending. It should sound like Xavier and remain brief enough to invite a discussion.

Avoid diagnosing CargoEmpowered in the opening. A useful later question is: “What did you learn from CargoEmpowered after I left?” Follow with what Alan would preserve, change, or stop doing. Xavier can describe what he now examines earlier: audience, actual behavior, alternatives, acquisition, economics, and acceptance criteria. None of this requires claiming that AI would have saved the previous application.

The previous departure also creates a practical continuity question. Xavier should decide his available capacity, the length of an initial commitment, working-hour overlap, communication expectations, and how his owned projects will fit around client work. A documented handover standard and transparent capacity are stronger reassurance than an unlimited-availability promise.

**5. Applying $100M Offers to this relationship**

Hormozi’s value framework connects the desired result and confidence in achieving it with the waiting time and effort required of the buyer. His offer-creation checklist begins with the outcome, identifies obstacles throughout the experience, develops solutions, and prioritizes their value relative to delivery cost. These are useful offer-design heuristics, not a formula that predicts whether Alan will buy.[15][16]

The following application is this report’s recommendation:

| Value lever | What Alan might value | How Xavier can make it concrete |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Desired result | An important initiative moves forward with accountable ownership | Choose one business priority and define the evidence that would count as progress |
| Confidence | Trust that Xavier can exercise judgment and finish the agreed work | Show an Armonía decision-to-adoption example, a Vela delivery example, and weekly inspectable work |
| Waiting time | Early useful evidence and visible progress | Timebox the highest-risk question and show a working flow or decision early |
| Buyer effort | Less translating ideas, coordinating contributors, and checking status | Xavier owns preparation, synthesis, the work plan, coordination, verification, and follow-through |

The offer must solve obstacles around delivery as well as delivery itself. For example, if Alan has to write every requirement, review every agent output, produce the marketing brief, and chase the release, the engagement has left much of his work intact.

| Potential obstacle to buying | Proposed response |
| --- | --- |
| “I can already use AI.” | Demonstrate ownership of the entire workstream and the decisions connecting its parts |
| “We already have developers.” | Define how Xavier works with the existing technical lead and team, with clear responsibility for the selected initiative |
| “I will still need to manage everything.” | Agree on delegated decisions, concise escalation criteria, and a predictable progress review |
| “We might build the wrong thing.” | Test the uncertain assumption before committing the main build budget |
| “The scope will keep growing.” | Freeze the first outcome; every addition changes scope, timing, or budget explicitly |
| “The launch will become another project for me.” | Include the relevant adoption or distribution asset and the learning handoff in the same scope |
| “What if the relationship stops?” | Keep decisions, source work, access ownership, and handover material usable by the company |

Offer components should earn their place by removing one of these obstacles. A decision brief, demo, launch asset, measurement plan, and handover pack can form a coherent package. Adding unrelated services and assigning them inflated bonus values would weaken the clarity of this particular sale.

**6. Candidate first initiatives**

These are discussion hypotheses, not a proposed application backlog. Alan’s priorities and internal evidence determine the selection. A useful selection starts with his ideas; the examples below help make the possible role tangible.

| Candidate | Example bounded outcome | Evidence needed first | Adoption or distribution | Decision signal |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| One operational workflow improvement | Reduce the work required to turn one relocation request into a complete coordinator-ready case | Staff walkthrough, current steps, representative cases, handling time, existing tools and integrations | Staff walkthrough video, short operating guide, pilot with a named coordinator | Handling time, corrections, and repeated use relative to the current process |
| One new product or service opportunity | Resolve the riskiest question behind one of Alan’s ideas and produce a tested first workflow when warranted | Buyer, user, trigger, alternative, reachable participants, commercial logic | Demo and a channel-specific invitation through an available customer or partner route | Meaningful trial use, a pilot commitment, a paid signal where appropriate, or a justified stop |
| One existing-product adoption problem | Help a defined employee or supplier group complete a selected task more reliably | Current funnel or workflow, user complaints, support cases, relevant product access | In-product guidance, onboarding material, customer-success introduction | Completion, time to first useful action, support demand, and user feedback |
| One commercial proof and conversion path | Connect a specific offer to a useful demonstration and qualified prospect conversation | Current positioning, sales objections, traffic sources, customer evidence, sales ownership | Product demo, landing-page experiment, sales follow-up asset, relevant LinkedIn content | Qualified meetings and movement into the next sales step, with denominators recorded |

The default recommendation is to look first for an existing priority with a clear user, accessible evidence, a named internal sponsor, and a short path to observing the result. This could be a new product if Alan already has qualified interest. It could be an internal workflow if operational friction is more urgent. “Internal” is not automatically safer or easier; access and integration can make it harder.

For a coordinator example, begin by watching how requests are assembled. If unstructured messages are a material burden, an initial assisted workflow might propose structured case details, flag missing information, and prepare a draft next action for human review. If the existing software already solves that need, investigate configuration or adoption. A new AI service is justified only if it improves the job relative to the simpler alternative.

For a new-product example, define one buyer and one job. Compare the proposed product with today’s workaround and available alternatives. A prototype may be enough to test usability; a concierge service may better test demand; a working slice may be necessary to test an integration. Choose the smallest mechanism that answers the actual uncertainty. A broad consumer moving marketplace would require separate evidence about acquisition, supply, trust, fulfillment, and economics.

For marketing, Vaca’s Instagram workflow is evidence of an adaptable creative method. Alan’s initial route may be existing clients, employer relationships, suppliers, a sales demonstration, or LinkedIn. The channel should follow the intended buyer. A compelling film belongs in the scope when it helps explain or distribute the product; it should not displace the first usable workflow.

Before selecting a candidate, require a concrete pain or opportunity, sufficient access, someone responsible for adoption, a bounded first result, a feasible evaluation, and a budget owner. Compare eligible candidates on business relevance, strength of evidence, time to learning, dependence on Alan, implementation risk, and reusable value. Use a qualitative ranking until real inputs support scoring.

**7. Shape of the engagement**

Lead with the intended ongoing partnership and give it a bounded first chapter. A possible arrangement is a paid initial month focused on one priority, followed by a mutual continuation decision. “One month” is a proposed commercial container, not a promise to research, build, release, and validate any application in thirty days.

The initial outcome must match the uncertainty. If discovery is still needed, the deliverable can be a well-supported investment decision with a tested prototype. If the opportunity and requirements are already understood, the deliverable can be a working slice and an adoption test. A production release belongs in the commitment only when dependencies and acceptance requirements support it.

| Responsibility | Xavier | Alan and Mobility Empowered |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Priority | Recommend which initiative deserves attention and why | Choose the business priority and acceptable investment |
| Research | Prepare, conduct agreed sessions, synthesize, and propose decisions | Supply context and access; connect relevant staff or customers |
| Product and design | Own the coherent user flow, scope, and acceptance criteria | Resolve material commercial and domain tradeoffs |
| AI and engineering | Coordinate tasks, implement or work with the team, review evidence, and verify the result | Provide technical constraints; retain approval over material architecture and company risk |
| Launch and adoption | Prepare the relevant assets, rollout plan, training, and measurement | Approve external claims and release; assign business adoption ownership |
| Continuing operation | Document support, ownership, known limits, and the next recommendation | Agree who supports the system and authorize ongoing scope |

Use one active delivery priority during the initial engagement. Keep the future idea list small and reviewed. A possible rhythm is an initial context session, one short weekly decision-and-demo meeting, and written progress showing what changed, what was learned, and what requires Alan’s input. The meeting load should be agreed after seeing the initiative. It is not a guaranteed ceiling on Alan’s participation.

Commercially, choose a fixed fee for a tightly bounded first deliverable or a monthly fee for an explicit capacity allocation and workstream. Determine the price after confirming Xavier’s availability, the delivery responsibility, required access, external costs, and the value of the problem. Maintain an internal effort and cost estimate so the offer is sustainable. AI efficiency does not by itself determine the price.

A business-value discussion can use operational inputs: affected cases per month, minutes spent per case, frequency and cost of rework, and the relevant staff capacity. Saved time is recovered capacity unless it actually reduces cash expenditure. For commercial work, use qualified opportunities and expected contribution from additional business with attribution limits. Do not invent Mobility Empowered’s figures or treat all product improvement as immediate revenue.

Hormozi’s guarantee material presents several forms of risk reversal, including conditional service remedies and release from commitment. For this engagement, the recommended adaptation is limited initial commitment, reviewable milestones, company-usable deliverables, and a written remedy for an unmet acceptance criterion within Xavier’s control. The precise remedy, correction window, payment treatment, and dependency terms belong in a later proposal. Do not promise commercial success or unlimited free work.[17]

**8. Proof package for the conversation**

Prepare a compact package that Alan can absorb quickly. It should connect Xavier’s evidence to the job being proposed rather than make Alan infer that connection from four repositories.

1. A one-page partnership brief explaining the responsibility Xavier can take, how he works with Alan, and the intended first step.
2. A concise Armonía walkthrough: operating problem, important decision, usable product, adoption evidence, public launch material, and Xavier’s exact contribution. Use seeded or approved sanitized visuals.
3. One Vela example showing how a product question became a decision, specification, implemented behavior, and verification. Keep internal issue counts and governance terminology in supporting material.
4. A small optional appendix containing the store’s opportunity/cost comparison and Vaca’s brand-to-content example. They show range while preserving their experimental status.
5. A one-page working model covering capacity, decision rights, review rhythm, definition of done, and continuity.

An initial narrated walkthrough can be three to five minutes if the selected material supports that length. A new speculative application is not a prerequisite for reconnecting. A bespoke sample becomes worthwhile once Alan identifies a live priority.

If credible measured outcomes can be gathered before the conversation, add them with their baseline and source. Otherwise use the adoption evidence already available. Do not delay outreach for an exhaustive portfolio rebuild. Confirm the exact assets and claims that can be shared; private source documents remain supporting research.

**10. Questions for the conversation with Alan**

Start with Alan’s agenda. The most valuable questions concern recent events and actual work:

- Which initiative would you most like to move forward over the next few months?
- What happened the last time you tried to move it forward?
- Which parts currently depend on you, and which would you be comfortable handing over?
- Who experiences the problem, and what do they do today?
- What have customers or colleagues already asked for, used, rejected, or paid for?
- What does the existing product or team already cover?
- What would meaningful progress look like at our first review?
- What did you learn from CargoEmpowered after I left?
- Who else should help define the result, approve it, and support it afterward?
- What capacity and commercial arrangement would fit the company?

Do not force the discussion into a preselected AI application. Alan may need help with an existing roadmap, a new opportunity, a specific operational workflow, or coordinating a delivery already underway. All can support the desired partnership if the responsibility and value are real.

If he sees Xavier mainly as a developer, show the Armonía chain of decisions and ask for a first initiative with broader responsibility. If he already has product leadership, explore a complementary workstream rather than duplicate the role. If there is no budget or no willingness to delegate, record that honestly; a more elaborate pitch cannot substitute for those conditions.

**11. How to evaluate whether the offer worked**

The immediate objective of reconnecting is a substantive conversation and a shared understanding of a current priority. The next objective is a paid first initiative with clear responsibility. The longer objective is a durable working relationship in which Alan trusts Xavier to make ordinary decisions and carry work through its consequences.

Track the partnership as well as the product. At the first review, assess whether Alan had to repeatedly rewrite requirements, chase status, reconcile contributors, or redo decisions. Compare those observations with the agreed working model. Track blocked days, correction work, changed scope, and the usability of the handover. These measures support learning; they should not be presented as guaranteed improvements before a baseline exists.

For the product, define one primary result and a few guardrails before the test. A workflow pilot might track handling time with correction rate and staff acceptance. An adoption experiment might track task completion with support demand. A new-product test might track relevant use or credible pilot commitments. A commercial experiment might track qualified meetings and next-step conversion. Preserve small samples and missing data rather than promote an encouraging anecdote into a business result.

The recommended decision is to pursue the right-hand relationship, introduce it through a specific ownership offer, and use one paid initiative to establish trust in the expanded role. The exact application should emerge from Alan’s priorities and evidence.
