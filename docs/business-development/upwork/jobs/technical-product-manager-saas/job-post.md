## Products and responsibilities

The founder already has developers and wants someone to connect business vision, product design, specifications, development, testing, and production.

The initial products are:

1. A translation and interpreting platform with client, partner, interpreter, assignment, availability, billing, payment, and workflow features. Planned work includes VoIP interpreting and tender intelligence.
2. A language-school management platform covering students, trainers, companies, courses, scheduling, documents, learning records, billing, and automation.

The role owns requirements, PRDs, functional specifications, user stories, acceptance criteria, user journeys, edge cases, API requirements, backlog priorities, dependencies, risks, coordination, functional testing, UAT, documentation, and release support. The client wants a product lead who challenges premature or unnecessarily complex requests.

5. Have you worked with Laravel/PHP or a similar backend architecture?
6. Do you have experience with VoIP, SIP, Asterisk, Twilio, or other telephony technologies? This is optional.
7. How do you currently use AI in your Product Management workflow?
8. How many hours per week are you available?
9. What time zone do you work in?
10. What is your hourly or daily rate?
