## What the client wants

ADU Build•Print will turn an existing construction implementation and certification program into guided software. The client already has manuals, SOPs, training, market and zoning methods, model and pricing information, and an Excel-based Local Cost Conversion method. Tiny Homes of Tucson will be the first operator.

The product should guide people through the work, track evidence and approvals, and connect to their existing business tools. The desired modules are:

1. Operator dashboard with implementation progress, tasks, alerts, and certification status.
2. Implementation workflows with owners, dates, uploads, dependencies, approvals, and gates.
3. Role-based academy with training, assignments, assessments, and completion tracking.
4. Market and jurisdiction records with zoning requirements, contacts, sources, and verification dates.
5. Product and model records with plans, specifications, options, assumptions, and prices.
6. Local Cost Conversion using baseline costs, local factors and quotes, overrides, sources, confidence, and margin calculations.
7. Controlled SOP and resource library connected to relevant roles and workflows.
8. A possible later knowledge assistant grounded in approved BuildPrint materials.
9. Individual and company certification with evidence and approvals.
10. Embedded analytics, potentially using Power BI later.

The discovery phase must decide what belongs in the Beta and what should wait. This list is the client's product direction, not our commitment to deliver every module immediately.

## Architecture and ownership expectations

Support separate companies, memberships, roles, permissions, company-specific configuration, and secure integration credentials. Preserve room for future billing.

GoHighLevel, JobTread, and QuickBooks Online are the initial preferred integrations. CompanyCam and Canibuild may follow. BuildPrint should retain its own workflows and data model so other providers can be supported. External learning and analytics platforms are options to assess.

The client expects personal delivery or a specifically named small team and ownership of the repositories, cloud accounts, credentials, database, and resulting work.

## First paid phase

The requested 20-40 hours should produce an architecture and data model, stack recommendation, roles and permissions, screen/module map, integration strategy, tenancy and security approach, Beta scope and deferrals, milestone estimates, and a clickable operator prototype.

## Required proposal opening

> I understand that Build•Print is an operating system, not a replacement for construction-management software.

## Questions to address in the proposal

1. How would you approach this Beta?
2. What technology stack would you recommend and why?
3. How would you make the architecture software-agnostic while still supporting preferred integrations?
4. How would you structure the multi-tenant data model?
5. What would you build in Beta versus defer?
6. What similar SaaS/API/integration projects have you personally built?
7. Who specifically would perform the work?
8. What would you estimate for the paid discovery/architecture phase?
9. What preliminary range would you expect for the functional Beta?
