# Upwork research and proposals

This folder is the durable record for Upwork research, shortlisted jobs, application drafts, and confirmed outcomes.

## Writing style

- Cut to the chase.
- Keep responses and drafts simple.
- Write like you are helping a friend, not preparing formal business copy.
- Use only the details needed to answer the client's question.
- Prefer concrete examples over broad claims.
- Do not use em dashes.

These rules apply to cover letters, screening answers, proposal drafts, tracker notes, daily research, Linear updates, and assistant replies about this work.

## Application privacy and client naming

- Never mention Armonía by name in an Upwork application, cover letter, screening answer, proposal attachment, or client message.
- Do not include an Armonía case-study link or another link that reveals the name.
- Make Xavier's established Upwork client history clear. Prefer direct wording such as "Most of my client work has come through Upwork".
- Always use wording such as "in my last Upwork project...", whenever the Upwork job post needs an example of a previous experience in whichever technology or tool.
- Internal evidence notes may retain the source project name so claims can still be checked, but application-facing copy must not expose it.

## After a confirmed application

When Xavier confirms that an application was submitted:

1. Save or update the job brief and proposal record under `jobs/`.
2. Mark the opportunity `Applied` in the opportunity tracker. Do not infer the exact submitted rate, copy, boost, or answers unless Xavier confirms them or the submitted proposal is read back.
3. Add the requested technologies and capabilities to the technology-signal register.
4. Separate verified experience, adjacent experience, and proof gaps. A technology named by a client is demand evidence, not evidence that Xavier has used it.
5. Record candidate portfolio projects or blog posts that could create honest proof. Do not start or publish an artifact without a separate selection decision.
6. If the proposal used an attribution alias, update the private attribution record from `reserved` to `distributed`. Record the confirmed send time, exact links actually sent, confirmation source, and actual Connect cost. Never infer these fields from the saved draft.
7. Keep reply, interview, and hire evidence as separate dated outcomes. Analytics activity cannot change an opportunity status.

Prefer one coherent product artifact that covers several repeated signals over unrelated technology demos. Prioritize signals that recur across applications, strengthen the approved positioning, or close a named proof gap.
