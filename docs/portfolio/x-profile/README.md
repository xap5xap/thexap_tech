## Profile copy ideas

The site and profile should speak to founders and small product teams. Keep the profile's first impression clear about the person, audience, and kind of work. The banner can carry the broader, more inspiring line while the bio names the working role.

### Display name draft

> Xavier Pérez | Product Engineer

### Bio draft

```text
🛠️ Senior full-stack product engineer
🎯 From unclear product work to dependable releases
🤝 Independent partner for founders & teams
👇 Work + contact
```

This is a proposed line-broken version of the approved portfolio positioning. Recheck the character count in X's editor before using it.

### Pinned post draft

```text
Make the ambitious real.

I help founders and small teams move an opportunity, prototype, or live product toward a coherent next release. Product judgment and full-stack delivery in one direct partnership.

Selected work: https://www.thexap.com/projects
```

Use a pinned post to connect the banner's broad promise to inspectable work. Review the exact wording and destination before publishing.

### Feed ideas

- Show a real product situation, a decision, the artifact, and what was verified or remains unknown.
- Use client-safe screenshots and working product surfaces where approved, rather than relying only on abstract diagrams.
- Try a sustainable posting rhythm and judge it by useful conversations or qualified visits, not follower count alone.

These are editorial directions, not measured growth claims or a posting schedule commitment.
